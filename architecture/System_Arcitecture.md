## Bus Architecture

The 96VDC Bus is the primary system energy bus.

All energy production, storage, and auxiliary power modules interface directly with the 96VDC bus.

The 380VDC Bus is a dedicated inverter supply bus.

Energy is transferred from the 96VDC bus to the 380VDC bus exclusively through one or more Boost Converter Nodes.

The 380VDC bus shall not be used for energy storage or energy source interconnection.

System Philosophy:

96VDC Bus = Energy Management

380VDC Bus = Power Conversion
