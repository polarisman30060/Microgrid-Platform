# 10 kW 2-Phase Interleaved Full-Bridge LLC Boost Module Hardware Baseline

## Module Scope
- **Operational Role:** Galvanically isolates and steps up the negative-grounded `96VDC Energy Bus` (`±5%` regulation) to the earth-referenced **`400VDC` (`±200VDC` split-rail) Inverter Bus** (`380.0 V – 415.0 V` normal) at **10 kW continuous** (`12–15 kW` short-term peak) per module.
- **Topology:** **2-phase (`2 × 5 kW`) interleaved Full-Bridge LLC (FB-LLC) resonant converter** with **`90°` primary phase interleaving** (`180°` output ripple cancellation at `2× f_sw`) and **center-tapped full-bridge SiC Schottky diode rectifiers** tied directly to the split-capacitor midpoint (`HV_MID = 0 V = AC Neutral = PE`). See the full engineering breakdown in [`Boost_Converter_Architecture.md`](./Boost_Converter_Architecture.md).
- **Control Architecture:** Local **STM32G474QET6** 4-layer daughter card running `HRTIM1` across 8 complementary outputs (`CHA1/2`, `CHB1/2` for Phase 1; `CHC1/2`, `CHD1/2` for Phase 2) with Pulse-Frequency Modulation (`PFM`), Phase-Shift Modulation (`PSM`) soft-start and active Phase 1 / Phase 2 current-balance trim, light-load single-phase shedding, and `HRTIM1` hardware burst mode, paired with the common control card's Waveshare `1.3-inch` SPI LCD + keypad HAT ([STM32_Control_Card_Architecture.md](../STM32_control_card/STM32_Control_Card_Architecture.md)).
- **Communication:** Galvanically isolated **CANbus** (`STM32G474QE` `FDCAN` interface, `500 kbps` Classic CAN via `TI ISO1042DWVR` on the common control card) supporting master/slave current-matching load balancing and Coordinator telemetry per [`../../protocol/CAN_Messages_Definitions.md`](../../protocol/CAN_Messages_Definitions.md) (`TELEM_BOOST_FAST` `0x300+` at `50 ms`, `TELEM_BOOST_SLOW` `0x310+` at `1 s`).

## Power Stage & Semiconductors
- **Resonant & Switching Frequency:** Nominal resonant frequency **`f_r = 45.0 kHz`** (`~38–65 kHz` PFM operating band; `180 kHz` effective combined output ripple frequency across the two `90°`-interleaved full-bridge phases; `< 28 kHz` capacitive-mode floor). Engineered for generous `±15–20%` DIY hand-winding and busbar-parasitic fudge factor.
- **Zero-PCB-Power Mechanical Standard:** All `12×` power semiconductor modules are baseplate-isolated **SOT-227 (miniBLOC)** packages bolted flat to one grounded aluminum heatsink, interconnected exclusively by **M4/M6 stacked copper busbars** (`1/8" × 1"` laminated `96 V+ / 96 V-` with `0.5 mm` Nomex/G10 insulator) and crimped copper lugs. The 2-layer `1 oz` host PCB carries `< 1 A` of gate-drive and sensor signals only.
- **Primary Power Switches (`8×` — `4×` per phase):** Baseplate-isolated **IXYS/Littelfuse `IXFN300N20X3`** (`200 V`, `300 A`, `~3.5 mΩ`, DigiKey `IXFN300N20X3-ND`; high-stock alt `IXFN230N20T`) MOSFETs in **SOT-227** packages with dedicated **Gate (`G`) and Kelvin Source (`KS`)** screw terminals.
- **Secondary Rectifiers (`4×` dual SOT-227 modules — `2×` per phase):** **SemiQ `GHXS100B065S-D3`** dual isolated `650 V` SiC Schottky diode arrays in **SOT-227** packages wired as center-tapped full-bridge rectifiers (`4` diodes per phase feeding `+200 VDC` and `-200 VDC` around `HV_MID`).
- **Gate Drivers:** Four (4) **TI `UCC21520DWR`** (`UCC21520ADWR`) dual-channel isolated gate drivers (`5.7 kVrms`, `4 A / 6 A`, hardware shoot-through interlock) + `8×` `Murata NXJ2S2415MC-R7` isolated bias bricks (`+12.3 V / -2.7 V`) on the 2-layer signal PCB, wired via short twisted pairs to each SOT-227 `G`/`KS` terminal, with shared `GATE_EN` (`PD15`) and dual-path `< 300 ns` hardware fault latch kill (`HRTIM1_FLT1` + direct driver `DISABLE`). See [`Boost_Converter_BOM.md`](./Boost_Converter_BOM.md).

## Magnetics, Resonant Tank & Split Bulk Capacitance
- **HF Isolation Transformers (`2×`, `T1`/`T2`):** Single **TDK/Epcos `U93/76/30`** (`N87` ferrite, giant `1,520 mm²` window, `11%` copper fill!) per phase, reinforced isolation (`≥ 4 kVrms`), turns ratio **`N_p : N_s1 : N_s2 = 6 : 13 : 13`** (`4.333` step-up, `B_pk = 106 mT` @ `45 kHz`), uniform **`1.52 mm` 2-leg FR4/Nomex shim** for **`L_m = 12.50 µH`**, hand-wound with easy DIY **`26 AWG` twisted bundles** (`r = 0.202 mm < 0.311 mm` skin depth at `45 kHz`: `4× 30-strand` primary + bifilar `25-strand` secondary halves).
- **Resonant Tanks (`2×`):** Per phase: total loop resonant inductance **`L_r = 2.08 µH`** (`~0.8–1.3 µH` transformer leakage + busbar stray + `~0.8–1.2 µH` LCR-tuned external gapped `PQ40/40` / `E55` ferrite inductor, matched `±1.5%` between phases) and busbar-mounted series resonant capacitor bank **`C_r = 6.00 µF`** (`4× 1.5 µF / 700–1000 VDC` screw-lug MKP pulse film capacitors bolted directly between `SW_A` and `L_r` copper bars — zero PCB current).
- **400 VDC (`±200 VDC`) Centralized Split Bulk Capacitance:** Separate external split bulk capacitor board (`60 mF` per half-rail = `30 mF` effective @ `400 V`, `12× 10,000 µF / 250 V` screw-terminal cans + polypropylene film bypass) enabling ultra-slow `3–5 Hz` outer voltage loops through `10 kW` transients, fitted with dual-gated fail-safe spring-closed `NC` `24 VDC` discharge relays (`100 Ω / 200 W` per rail, `< 9 s` to `< 48 VDC`, `0 W` running loss), backup `33 kΩ / 5 W` bleeders + HV LEDs, and the single-point `HV_MID → PE` (AC Neutral) bonding stud. See [`../../docs/Bus_Capacitance_Droop_and_Transient_Spec.md`](../../docs/Bus_Capacitance_Droop_and_Transient_Spec.md).

## Isolation, Protection & Telemetry
- **Inherent DC Fault Blocking & Fusing:** Series resonant capacitors (`C_r`) and transformers block DC fault current immediately upon primary gate inhibit (`HRTIM1_FLT1`, `< 1 µs`); protected by a `175–200 A` Class-T input fuse on `LV_BUS+` and 2-pole `50 A / 500 VDC` semiconductor output fuses on `HV+` and `HV-` (never fuse `HV_MID`).
- **Bus Interface & Auxiliary Power:** Standardized `24 VDC` coil Pre-Charge Bypass Contactor (`Q_PRE` + `22 Ω / 50 W` resistor for local `~6 mF` input cap) and hermetically sealed DC Main Isolation Contactor (`Q_ISOL`, e.g., `EV200`/`GX14`) powered from E-Stop-interlocked **`AUX_COIL_24V`**, plus un-interruptible control logic power from **`AUX_LOGIC_24V`** (Split Diode-OR'd `24 VDC` auxiliary bus; see [`../../docs/System_Safety_and_Protection_Architecture.md`](../../docs/System_Safety_and_Protection_Architecture.md)).
- **Isolated Telemetry:** `96 VDC` input and `400 VDC` output bus currents captured via **Allegro `ACS37002`** Hall-effect sensors; Phase 1 and Phase 2 primary resonant tank currents sensed via `1:100` HF CTs / high-bandwidth Hall sensors (feeding `< 500 ns` hardware fault latch); `V_BUS96`, `V_HV+` (`+200 V`), and `V_HV-` (`-200 V`) sensed independently via **TI `AMC1311`** isolated differential amplifiers; heatsink/rectifier NTCs plus fail-safe magnetics thermal snap discs + `ESTOP_OK` (`HRTIM1_FLT3`).
